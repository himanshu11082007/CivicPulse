How Priority Is Calculated

Priority = 8S + 6V + 4A + 2T

S = safety risk; V = severity; A = affected-population category; T = waiting-time category.

Each factor ranges from 1 to 5. Maximum score: 100.

80–100: Critical · 60–79: High · 40–59: Medium · 20–39: Low
